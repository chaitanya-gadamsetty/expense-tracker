Expense Tracker

Know where your household's money goes, without typing every rupee.

A free web app for iPhone, Android and computers. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.

Open the app

Paste the bank SMS

Copy a debit, card or UPI message, add a word like “groceries”, and it fills in amount, date, merchant and category. You confirm before anything is saved.

Stay within budget

A monthly household budget, optional category budgets, and warnings at 80% and 100%. Spots unusual spikes too.

See the pattern

By category, by person, and Essential · Need · Want · Waste, month by month and across the year.

Share with your partner

Optional sync through one file in your own Google Drive. Each of you uses your own Google account.

Your data stays yours

  • Expenses are stored on your phone. There is no server and no account with us.
  • If you turn on sync, they also go to one file in your own Google Drive. The app can see only that file, nothing else in your Drive.
  • Only the last 4 digits of cards and accounts are kept. The SMS text and the names of people you pay by UPI are not stored.

Read the full privacy policy

Install it like an app

  1. iPhone: open this page in Safari → tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome → tap Install app (or ⋮ → Add to Home screen).
  3. Computer: use it in Chrome, Edge or Safari; Chrome can also install it from the address bar.

Installing keeps your data safer on iPhone: Safari may clear data for sites that are only opened in a tab and not used for weeks.
