Expense Tracker

Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

In short: your expenses live on your own devices and, if you turn on sync, in one file in your own Google Drive. There is no Expense Tracker server, no account with us, no ads and no analytics. We never see your data.

Who we are

Expense Tracker is a free personal project by Chaitanya Gadamsetty, served from chaitanya-gadamsetty.github.io/expense-tracker. Contact: chaitu.others@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

  • On your device (in your browser's storage): the expenses you add (amount, date, category, merchant, spend type, who paid, payment method, the last 3–4 digits of a card or account, and any note you type), your categories, budgets, recurring bills and household names.
  • Not stored: the full text of SMS you paste, full card or account numbers, and the names of people you pay by UPI (they are replaced with “UPI”).
  • Backups you download are files on your device; you decide where they go.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, you sign in with Google and the app asks for one permission: drive.file, which lets it create and use files you create with it or pick in Google's file picker. With it the app:

  • creates an “Expense Tracker” folder and one household data file in your Drive, plus up to 7 daily copies of that file;
  • reads and updates that file to keep your devices in step;
  • shares that file with an email address only when you type it and tap Share;
  • reads your Google account email address, only to show which account is connected and to skip the account chooser next time.

The app cannot see any other file in your Drive. Data travels directly between your device and Google; it does not pass through any server of ours. Your sign-in lasts about an hour and is kept only in your browser.

Expense Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide sync for you and the people you share with; it is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models, and no human reads it.

Other services involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. Like any website host, it may log your IP address when you load the app. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google handles sign-in, Drive and the file picker when you use sync. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Voice entry uses your browser's or keyboard's speech recognition, which may send audio to Apple or Google to turn it into text. The app only receives the text.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Edit or delete any expense in the app at any time.
  • Delete everything on a device by clearing this site's data in your browser settings (or deleting the home-screen app's data).
  • Delete the synced copy by deleting the “Expense Tracker” folder in your Google Drive.
  • Stop sync with Disconnect this phone, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing household money and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
